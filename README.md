### 🏢 About Upnex IT

Welcome to the official GitHub organization profile of **Upnex IT** — a forward-thinking software engineering and IT innovation studio founded by **[Mehedi Hasan](https://github.com/Mehedi-dev26)**, based in **Naogaon, Rajshahi, Bangladesh**.

We specialize in designing and engineering high-availability cloud platforms, intelligent multi-tenant SaaS suites, custom ERP systems, and modern digital ecosystems for businesses, institutions, and enterprises across Bangladesh and beyond.

#### 🎯 Our Core Mission & Capabilities
- ⚡ **High-Velocity Product Engineering**: Rapidly transforming complex business challenges into production-ready, beautifully designed digital products.
- 🏬 **Retail & SaaS Transformation**: Powering retail chains, supermarkets, electronics showrooms, and installment enterprises with **Upit-POS**.
- 🏫 **Institutional & ERP Systems**: Developing bespoke School Management, Hospital & Diagnostic Management, and supply chain ERP architectures.
- 🔒 **Zero-Trust Security & Data Integrity**: Implementing kernel-level Row Level Security (RLS), immutable audit trails, and strict multi-tenant isolation.
- 🌐 **Modern Tech Stacks**: Mastering reactive client interfaces, real-time WebSocket feeds, and resilient cloud databases.
